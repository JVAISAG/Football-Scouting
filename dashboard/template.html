Every decision has a definition

Methods & data.

The analytical framework

  • Business question: Which historical attacking players should a club prioritize for role-specific video review?
  • Data: a complete 2015/16 Premier League season from StatsBomb Open Data. All results are historical.
  • Pipeline: compressed source cache → normalized SQLite tables → non-penalty shot model → player metrics → role scores → constrained selection.
  • Model split: matches ordered by date, with entire dates kept together. Earlier training, middle validation, later testing.
  • Selection: choose lowest validation log loss, refit on training + validation, then evaluate on the held-out test period.
  • Two model artifacts: the evaluation model powers this lab; a separate full-season refit describes retrospective player shot quality.

How the shortlist is scored

  • At least 900 regulation minutes and at least 25% of time in the role.
  • Rates per 90 minutes are shrunk toward a role-average rate using a 450-minute prior.
  • Weighted percentiles are computed within eligible roles. Weights are analyst choices, not learned measures of true player value.
  • Striker: 45% npxG, 20% box shots, 15% npxA, 10% progressive carries, 10% pressures.
  • Winger: 25% npxG, 30% npxA, 20% progressive carries, 15% progressive passes, 10% pressures.
  • Creator: 35% npxA, 25% key passes, 25% progressive passes, 10% npxG, 5% pressures.

Metric dictionary

  • npxG: summed predicted goal probabilities for non-penalty shots.
  • npxA: npxG of shots directly linked to a player's key passes.
  • Progressive pass: completed non-restart pass advancing at least 15 pitch units and ending in the attacking half.
  • Progressive carry: carry advancing at least 10 units and ending in the attacking half.
  • Box shot: shot with X ≥ 102 and Y between 18 and 62.
  • Minutes: position intervals capped at 45 minutes per half; stoppage-time events are retained.

Limits worth discussing

  • One historical season gives no evidence of present-day player ability or availability.
  • Scouting scores and full-season xG are descriptive and in-sample; future player performance has not been validated.
  • Team tactics, opponent quality, possession, injuries and wages are not controlled.
  • Pressure flags and other event annotations depend on provider collection practices.
  • The recruitment objective assumes role scores are additive. It does not model player chemistry or a full XI.
  • Model calibration and probability error matter more than headline classification accuracy.
